# O-RAN Deployment Papers

> Representative papers on O-RAN deployment strategies, testbeds, and operational experience. Each entry: title, authors/affiliations, year, venue, and a one-sentence abstract. Unverified items are marked [需核实].

## 1. Colosseum: Large-Scale Wireless Experimentation Through Hardware-in-the-Loop Network Emulation

- **Authors**: Leonardo Bonati, Pedram Johari, Michele Polese, Salvatore D'Oro, Soheil Mohanti, et al. (Northeastern University)
- **Year / Venue**: 2021, IEEE DySPAN
- **Summary**: Describes Colosseum, the world's largest RF emulator, enabling large-scale O-RAN experimentation with hardware-in-the-loop.

## 2. OpenRAN Gym: AI/ML Development, Data Collection, and Testing for O-RAN on PAWR Platforms

- **Authors**: Leonardo Bonati, Michele Polese, Salvatore D'Oro, Stefano Basagni, Tommaso Melodia
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, Computer Networks, vol. 220, pp. 1–11
- **Summary**: Open-source experimental toolbox for end-to-end AI/ML design, data collection, and testing on O-RAN-compatible PAWR testbeds.

## 3. X5G: An Open, Programmable, Multi-Vendor Private 5G O-RAN Testbed

- **Authors**: Davide Villa, Leonardo Bonati, et al. [需核实]
- **Year / Venue**: 2023/2024, IEEE Network [需核实]
- **Summary**: 8-node multi-vendor private 5G O-RAN testbed with NVIDIA ARC-OTA and OAI, used for real-world RIC/AI deployment studies.

## 4. Colosseum: The Open RAN Digital Twin

- **Authors**: Michele Polese, Salvatore D'Oro, Leonardo Bonati, Tommaso Melodia et al.
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, IEEE Communications Magazine
- **Summary**: Presents Colosseum as an O-RAN digital twin for pre-deployment validation of AI/ML RAN control policies.

## 5. ns-O-RAN: Simulating O-RAN 5G Systems in ns-3

- **Authors**: A. Lacava, M. Bordin, M. Polese, R. Sivaraj, T. Zugno, F. Cuomo, T. Melodia
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, ACM WNS3 2023
- **Summary**: Open-source ns-3 simulator integrating a near-RT RIC with a simulated RAN, enabling safe pre-deployment RAN algorithm validation.

## 6. ORANSlice: An Open-Source 5G Network Slicing Platform for O-RAN

- **Authors**: H. Cheng, S. D'Oro, R. Gangula, S. Velumani, D. Villa, L. Bonati, M. Polese, T. Melodia, G. Arrobo, C. Maciocco
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, ACM MobiCom'24 Workshop on Open and AI RAN
- **Summary**: End-to-end open-source 5G network slicing framework for O-RAN, validated on Arena and X5G testbeds.

## 7. ColoRAN: Developing and Testing Deep Learning-based Solutions for Open RAN in Colosseum

- **Authors**: [需核实]
- **Year / Venue**: 2022, IEEE ICC [需核实]
- **Summary**: Early demonstration of deep-learning-based RAN control solutions developed and tested on the Colosseum O-RAN testbed.

## 8. TIMESAFE: Timing Interruption Monitoring and Security Assessment for Fronthaul Environments

- **Authors**: Joshua Groen, Simone Di Valerio, Imtiaz Karim, Davide Villa, et al.
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, arXiv [需核实]
- **Summary**: Assesses timing interruptions in deployed O-RAN fronthaul environments, highlighting operational challenges.

## 9. Open RAN xApps Design and Evaluation: Lessons Learnt and Identified Challenges

- **Authors**: M. Hoffmann et al.
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, IEEE JSAC, vol. 42, no. 2
- **Summary**: Deployment and evaluation lessons from running real xApps on near-RT RIC infrastructure, covering practical integration challenges.
