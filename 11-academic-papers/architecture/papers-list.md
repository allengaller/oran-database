# O-RAN Architecture Papers

> Representative papers on O-RAN architectural design, disaggregation, and virtualized/cloud-native RAN. Each entry: title, authors/affiliations, year, venue, and a one-sentence abstract. Unverified items are marked [需核实].

## 1. Understanding O-RAN: Architecture, Interfaces, Algorithms, Security, and Research Challenges

- **Authors**: Michele Polese, Leonardo Bonati, Salvatore D'Oro, Stefano Basagni, Tommaso Melodia (Northeastern University)
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials, vol. 25, no. 2, pp. 1376–1411
- **Summary**: The canonical reference on O-RAN architecture: CU/DU/RU splits, RICs, and interfaces, with architectural design rationale.

## 2. Empowering the 6G Cellular Architecture with Open RAN

- **Authors**: Michele Polese, Mischa Dohler, Falko Dressler, Melike Erol-Kantarci, Rittwik Jana, Raymond Knopp, Tommaso Melodia et al.
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications (JSAC), vol. 42, no. 2, pp. 245–262
- **Summary**: Positions Open RAN as the architectural foundation of 6G, discussing disaggregation and programmability across the RAN stack.

## 3. Open, Programmable, and Virtualized 5G Networks: State-of-the-Art and the Road Ahead

- **Authors**: Leonardo Bonati, Michele Polese, Salvatore D'Oro, Stefano Basagni, Tommaso Melodia (Northeastern University)
- **Year / Venue**: 2020, Elsevier Computer Networks
- **Summary**: Pre-O-RAN-ALLIANCE landscape of open/programmable/virtualized 5G architectures that motivated and shaped the O-RAN design.

## 4. Intelligence and Learning in O-RAN for Data-Driven NextG Cellular Networks

- **Authors**: Salvatore D'Oro, Leonardo Bonati, Michele Polese, Tommaso Melodia (Northeastern University)
- **Year / Venue**: 2021, IEEE Communications Magazine [卷期需核实]
- **Summary**: Early vision of embedding intelligence into the O-RAN architecture through the RIC and closed control loops.

## 5. O-RAN Architecture Description (O-RAN.WG1.OAD)

- **Authors**: O-RAN Alliance WG1
- **Year / Venue**: O-RAN Alliance Technical Specification, updated regularly (e.g., v09, 2024)
- **Summary**: Normative specification of the O-RAN reference architecture — O-CU, O-DU, O-RU, near-RT/non-RT RIC, and SMO.
