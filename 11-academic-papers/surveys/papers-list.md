# O-RAN Survey Papers

> Representative survey and overview papers on the O-RAN technology landscape. Each entry: title, authors/affiliations, year, venue, and a one-sentence abstract. Entries that could not be fully verified are marked [需核实] (needs verification).

## 1. Understanding O-RAN: Architecture, Interfaces, Algorithms, Security, and Research Challenges

- **Authors**: Michele Polese, Leonardo Bonati, Salvatore D'Oro, Stefano Basagni, Tommaso Melodia (Northeastern University)
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials, vol. 25, no. 2, pp. 1376–1411
- **Summary**: The definitive O-RAN tutorial and survey covering the reference architecture, interfaces, algorithms, ML applications, security, and open research challenges; the most-cited entry point into O-RAN literature.

## 2. Empowering the 6G Cellular Architecture with Open RAN

- **Authors**: Michele Polese, Mischa Dohler, Falko Dressler, Melike Erol-Kantarci, Rittwik Jana, Raymond Knopp, Tommaso Melodia et al.
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications (JSAC), vol. 42, no. 2, pp. 245–262
- **Summary**: Argues Open RAN principles will be central to 6G, surveying how disaggregation, programmability, and intelligence reshape the cellular architecture.

## 3. Open RAN Security: Challenges and Opportunities

- **Authors**: Madhusanka Liyanage, An Braeken, Shahriar Shahabuddin, Pasika Ranaweera
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, Journal of Network and Computer Applications, vol. 214, 103621
- **Summary**: Surveys the O-RAN threat landscape introduced by openness and disaggregation, mapping security challenges to available countermeasures.

## 4. Deep Learning for B5G Open Radio Access Network: Evolution, Survey, Case Studies, and Challenges

- **Authors**: B. Brik, K. Boutiba, A. Ksentini
- **Year / Venue**: 2022, IEEE Open Journal of the Communications Society, vol. 3, pp. 228–250
- **Summary**: Surveys deep-learning techniques for B5G Open RAN use cases, with case studies and open challenges for ML-driven Open RAN.

## 5. A Brief Survey of Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN) Security

- **Authors**: arXiv preprint, author list [需核实]
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, arXiv:2311.02311
- **Summary**: Concise survey of the security threats posed by O-RAN disaggregation and the security mechanisms proposed by the O-RAN Alliance.
