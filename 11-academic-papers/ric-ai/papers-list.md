# O-RAN RIC and AI/ML Papers

> Representative papers on the RAN Intelligent Controller (RIC), xApps/rApps, and AI/ML algorithms in O-RAN. Each entry: title, authors/affiliations, year, venue, and a one-sentence abstract. Unverified items are marked [需核实].

## 1. Intelligence and Learning in O-RAN for Data-Driven NextG Cellular Networks

- **Authors**: Salvatore D'Oro, Leonardo Bonati, Michele Polese, Tommaso Melodia et al. (Northeastern University)
- **Year / Venue**: 2021, IEEE Communications Magazine
- **Summary**: Foundational vision of embedding ML intelligence into O-RAN via the RIC, detailing closed control loops at different timescales.

## 2. OrchestRAN: Orchestrating Network Intelligence in the Open RAN

- **Authors**: Salvatore D'Oro, Leonardo Bonati, Michele Polese, Tommaso Melodia et al. (Northeastern University)
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing
- **Summary**: Framework for orchestrating multiple intelligent closed control loops on the RIC while avoiding conflicts between concurrent xApps.

## 3. Open RAN xApps Design and Evaluation: Lessons Learnt and Identified Challenges

- **Authors**: M. Hoffmann et al.
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications (JSAC), vol. 42, no. 2, pp. 473–486
- **Summary**: Practical lessons and challenges in designing, deploying, and evaluating xApps on the near-RT RIC, based on real implementations.

## 4. A Comparative Analysis of Deep Reinforcement Learning-based xApps in O-RAN

- **Authors**: Maria Tsampazi, Salvatore D'Oro, Michele Polese, Leonardo Bonati, et al.
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, IEEE GLOBECOM 2023
- **Summary**: Comparative evaluation of DRL-based xApps for RAN control in O-RAN, examining training/inference trade-offs across DRL designs.

## 5. Intelligent Closed-loop RAN Control with xApps in OpenRAN Gym

- **Authors**: Leonardo Bonati, Michele Polese, Salvatore D'Oro, Stefano Basagni, Tommaso Melodia
- **Year / Venue**: 2022, European Wireless 2022
- **Summary**: Demonstrates closed-loop RAN control with xApps in the OpenRAN Gym experimental toolbox on real O-RAN infrastructure.

## 6. Programmable and Customized Intelligence for Traffic Steering in 5G Networks Using Open RAN Architectures

- **Authors**: A. Lacava, M. Polese, R. Sivaraj, T. Zugno, F. Cuomo, T. Melodia
- **Year / Venue**: 2023, IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing (vol. 23, no. 2, 2024) [需核实年份/卷期]
- **Summary**: Uses the RIC to steer traffic intelligently in 5G networks using O-RAN-based control, demonstrating programmable intelligence.

## 8. xDevSM: Streamlining xApp Development With a Flexible Framework for O-RAN E2 Service Models

- **Authors**: A. Feraudo, S. Maxenti, A. Lacava, P. Bellavista, M. Polese, T. Melodia
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, ACM workshop [需核实 venue]
- **Summary**: Framework streamlining xApp development with flexible E2 service model tooling for near-RT RIC integration.

## 9. Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning for Resource Allocation in O-RAN Slicing

- **Authors**: H. Zhang, H. Zhou, M. Erol-Kantarci
- **Year / Venue**: 2022, IEEE GLOBECOM 2022
- **Summary**: Applies federated deep reinforcement learning to distributed resource allocation in O-RAN slicing scenarios.

## 10. Towards Continuous Latency Monitoring through Open RAN Interfaces

- **Authors**: Satis Kumar Permal, Yixi Chen, Xin Zhe Khooi, Biqing Qiu, Mun Choon Chan
- **Year / Venue**: 2024, ACM Workshop on 5G and Beyond Network Measurements, Modeling, and Use Cases
- **Summary**: Shows how to leverage O-RAN interfaces for continuous latency monitoring in Open RAN deployments.

## 11. X5G: An Open, Programmable, Multi-Vendor Private 5G O-RAN Testbed

- **Authors**: Davide Villa, Leonardo Bonati, et al. [需核实]
- **Year / Venue**: 2023/2024, IEEE Network [需核实]
- **Summary**: Multi-vendor private 5G O-RAN testbed with NVIDIA ARC-OTA and OAI, enabling realistic RIC/AI experimentation.
