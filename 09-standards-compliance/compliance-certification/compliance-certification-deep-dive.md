# Compliance and Certification Deep Dive

## Overview

Compliance and certification are critical aspects of O-RAN deployments, ensuring that solutions meet industry standards, regulatory requirements, and interoperability expectations. This document provides a comprehensive exploration of compliance requirements, certification processes, testing laboratories, and compliance verification procedures for O-RAN deployments.

Understanding compliance and certification is crucial for vendors seeking to enter the O-RAN market, operators deploying O-RAN solutions, and organizations ensuring regulatory compliance. This document covers all aspects of O-RAN compliance and certification, from initial requirements to ongoing monitoring.
