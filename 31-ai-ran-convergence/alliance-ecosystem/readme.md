# AI-RAN Alliance & Ecosystem (2026)

> **Updated: 2026-05** | Sources: AI-RAN Alliance, MWC 2026, GTC 2026, Dell'Oro Group

## 1. AI-RAN Alliance Overview

### Mission
The **AI-RAN Alliance** was founded to advance mobile network performance through AI innovation, shaping future **AI-native networks**. As of 2026, it has become the central industry body driving the convergence of AI and RAN.

### Key Members (2026)
- **Founding/Platinum**: NVIDIA, SoftBank, T-Mobile US, Samsung, ARM, Ericsson
- **Strategic**: Nokia, NEC, Fujitsu, MIT, Northeastern University
- **Notable Absence**: Intel has chosen to sit out (as of MWC 2026), citing uncertainty about monetizable non-RAN workloads on GPU-based base stations

### Working Groups & Focus Areas
| Working Group | Focus Area | 2026 Priority |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **AI-for-RAN** | AI/ML for RAN optimization | Production validation of DRL xApps |
| **AI-on-RAN** | Edge AI service provisioning | Revenue model development |
| **AI-with-RAN** | Shared compute infrastructure | GPU resource partitioning standards |
| **AI-RAN Security** | Safety of autonomous AI in RAN | Guardrailing frameworks |
| **6G Research** | AI-native architecture design | Intrinsic AI principles |

### 2026 Milestones
1. **"AI-Native RAN: From White Papers to Validation"** (April 2026) — The Alliance announced a strategic shift from research-oriented white papers to **commercial validation and deployment**
2. **MWC Barcelona 2026 Demonstrations** — Multiple live demos showcasing AI-RAN in operator environments
3. **Partnership with O-RAN Alliance** — Joint efforts on specification alignment for AI/ML workflows

## 2. O-RAN Alliance AI/ML Specification Evolution (2026)

### Specification Releases

#### February 2026: 71 New or Updated Technical Documents
The largest specification release to date, including:
- **WG2 Non-RT RIC Architecture** updates
- **A1 Interface** enhancements for AI/ML policy management
- **R1 Interface** (RIC platform API) updates
- **UCR (Use Case Registry)** expansions
- Impact analysis and recommendations for Non-RT RIC deployments

#### Ongoing WG Activities for AI/ML

| Working Group | AI/ML Focus | 2026 Deliverables |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **WG2** (Non-RT RIC) | rApp architecture, A1 policy framework | Updated RIC architecture spec with AI/ML workflow support |
| **WG3** (Near-RT RIC) | xApp framework, E2 service models | Enhanced E2SM for ML-driven control loops |
| **WG7** (AI/ML) | End-to-end AI/ML lifecycle | AI/ML model management, training, deployment specs |
| **WG11** (Security) | Secure AI, Zero Trust | Post-Quantum Cryptography, Secure AI specifications |

### WG11 Security Focus Areas (2026)
The Security Working Group has identified four priority areas for 2026:
1. **Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA)** for AI-RAN components
2. **Secure AI** — ensuring AI/ML operations within O-RAN are protected
3. **Continuous Security Monitoring** for autonomous network agents
4. **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)** — preparing for 6G quantum threats

## 5. MWC 2026 Highlights

The Mobile World Congress Barcelona (March 2026) was a watershed moment for AI-RAN:

### Key Demonstrations
1. **Nokia + NVIDIA**: Live AI-with-RAN demo showing RAN and AI workloads on shared GPU
2. **SynaXG + Eridan**: Commercially-ready AI-RAN on shared CPU/GPU platform
3. **O-RAN Alliance Summit**: Operators discussing Open RAN at scale
4. **AI-RAN Alliance**: Multiple member demonstrations of AI-RAN integration

### Key Announcements
- Nokia's path to AI-native 6G through new partnerships
- T-Mobile's 2026 AI-RAN trial program
- O-RAN Alliance focus on specification consolidation for AI-driven networks
- Growing operator optimism about AI-RAN economics

### Intel's Absence
Notably, Intel chose to sit out the AI-RAN Alliance at MWC 2026, reflecting a broader industry debate:
- **Bull case**: GPUs at cell sites enable new revenue streams through edge AI
- **Bear case**: Without proven monetizable non-RAN workloads, GPU CAPEX is hard to justify
